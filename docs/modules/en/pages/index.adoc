= Handle a vulnerability report
:page-type: how-to
include::_fpsot-terms.adoc[]

This guide takes a member of the {abbr-fpsot-first} security team from a new report to the public release.
Each step cites the rule it meets.

== Before any report

Turn on the private vulnerability reporting of each repository, under SEC-064.
Copy the template `examples/SECURITY.md` into each repository and fill in its placeholders, under SEC-063.
Check that the security contacts of each project can reach the security team in private, under SEC-002.

== Day 0 to day 3: acknowledge

A report arrives through the private channel of SEC-040.
If it arrived in a public issue or in a Working Group, hide it at once and follow SEC-041.
Answer the reporter within 3 business days, under SEC-043.
Open a private record of the report, under SEC-048.

== Day 3 to day 14: assess

Reproduce the flaw on a supported version.
Score it with {abbr-cvss-first} v4.0 and record the full vector, under SEC-044.
For example, a flaw that lets any caller on the network read the data of others could score as follows.

[source,text]
----
CVSS:4.0/AV:N/AC:L/AT:N/PR:L/UI:N/VC:H/VI:N/VA:N/SC:N/SI:N/SA:N
----

Tell the reporter, within 10 business days, whether you accept the report and how severe it is.
Request a {abbr-cve-first} identifier for it, under SEC-045.
Note the deadline of the public release: 90 days after the report, under SEC-055.

== Fix in private

Write the fix in a temporary private fork, never in a public branch.
The second review of SEC-010 still applies.
Write the test of SEC-047, but keep it in the private fork until the public release, under SEC-083.
Prepare a patch release of each supported version that the flaw affects, under SEC-062.

== Send the pre-notice

Choose the head start, under SEC-054.
Seven days is the default.
Use 72 hours for a simple fix, and up to 14 days for a fix that needs integration testing.
Record the reason for any other value.

Send the pre-notice to every member of the list at the same time, under SEC-050 and SEC-053.
It holds the details, the fix, the vector, the affected versions and the date of the public release.
It also states the duties of members of SEC-057.

== Publish the date notice

About 7 days before the public release, publish the public date notice, under SEC-056.
It gives the date, the component and the severity, and nothing else.
When the head start is shorter, publish it with the pre-notice.

[source,text]
----
On 2026-12-08 at 14:00 UTC, FPSOT will publish a security fix for <component>.
Severity: High. Details will follow with the release.
----

== Watch for leaks

If a member reports a leak, or signs of exploitation appear, decide at once whether to release early, under SEC-055.
Remove from the list any member through which the pre-notice leaked, under SEC-058.

== Release

Publish the patch releases with their {abbr-sbom-first}, provenance and signatures, under SEC-033 to SEC-036.
Publish the security advisory in the {abbr-osv-first} format and in the advisory database of the forge, under SEC-046.
Credit the reporter as they wish.
Merge the test of the flaw, and look for its root cause, under SEC-047.
